A rounded-full segmented control. Two color variants seen in the source: a dark ("inverse") track for page-level tabs, and a cream ("light") track with a yellow active pill for panel-level tabs.

```jsx
<SegmentedTabs tabs={["Objectif","Notifications"]} variant="inverse" />
<SegmentedTabs tabs={["Compétences","Réalisations"]} variant="light" />
```
